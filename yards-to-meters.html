Why a yards time changes twice

Converting a swim time from yards to meters is not a unit conversion. You are not asking how far 100 yards is in metres — you are asking what the same swimmer would have done over a different distance, in a pool with a different number of walls. Both of those change the time, and they change it in opposite directions.

The distance

A yard is 0.9144 metres, so 100 yards is 91.44 metres. A 100-metre race is 8.56 metres longer — about nine per cent more water to cover. That alone makes the metres time slower.

The walls

Then there is the pool. A 25-yard pool and a 25-metre pool give the same number of turns over the same race, so converting between those two is pure distance — multiply by 1.11 and you are done.

A 50-metre pool is different. Over a 100 you get one turn instead of three, and a turn is a push-off and an underwater, both faster than swimming. So going to long course you add seconds back on top of the distance factor.

Doing it by hand

SCM = SCY × 1.11 — yards to short course metres, nothing else needed.

LCM = (SCY × 1.11) + turn value — yards to long course, with the walls added.

100 freestyle, 52.30 in yards. 52.30 × 1.11 = 58.05 SCM. Two walls lost at 0.8 s each adds 1.6 → 59.65 LCM.

The turn value depends on the stroke: 0.6 s per wall for backstroke, 0.7 for butterfly, 0.8 for freestyle and medley, and a full second for breaststroke. Going from metres back to yards, reverse the order — take the seconds off first, then divide by 1.11.

The distance events do not line up

This is where most conversions go wrong. In a yards pool the distance events are the 500, the 1000 and the 1650. In metres they are the 400, the 800 and the 1500. Those are not the same race, so they do not use 1.11.

Frequently asked questions

How do you convert swimming yards to meters?

Multiply your short course yards time by 1.11 to get short course metres. For long course metres, multiply by 1.11 and then add the turn value for your stroke — 1.6 seconds for a 100 freestyle. The distance events use 0.8925 or 1.02 instead.

How many meters is 100 yards in swimming?

91.44 metres, because a yard is exactly 0.9144 metres. That is why a 100-metre race takes longer than a 100-yard race even for the same swimmer at the same speed — it is 8.56 metres further.

Is a 25-yard pool the same as a 25-metre pool?

No. A 25-yard lane is 22.86 metres, so it is a little over two metres shorter. Both are short course and both give you the same number of turns, which is why converting between them is just the 1.11 factor with no turn adjustment.

Why is the factor 1.11 and not 1.0936?

Pure distance would give 1.0936, the ratio of a metre to a yard. The published factor is higher because a yards pool also hands you slightly more wall per 100 of racing, and those extra push-offs make yards times faster than distance alone explains. 1.11 absorbs both effects.

How do I convert meters back to yards?

Reverse the steps and the order. From long course metres, subtract the turn value first, then divide by 1.11. From short course metres, just divide by 1.11. The calculator does it either way — swap the two courses with the arrows.

Is a 500 yards the same as a 400 metres?

They are the equivalent races, but not the same distance. 500 yards is 457.2 metres, so a 400 metres is actually shorter — which is why that conversion uses 0.8925 and your metres time comes out faster than your yards time.
