About TimeSwimConvertor

What this site is, and where its numbers come from.

TimeSwimConvertor is a small set of free swimming calculators. There is no sign-up, no account, and nothing to install — every tool runs in your browser, and the times you type never leave your device.

What the tools do

  • Swim time converter — a time in 25-yard, 25-metre and 50-metre pools, side by side.
  • Long course to short course converter — one direction at a time, with the walls shown.
  • Swim pace calculator — pace per 100, speed, splits and a critical swim speed test.
  • FINA points calculator — the World Aquatics points score for any swim.
  • Laps in a mile — how many lengths a distance takes in your pool.

Where the numbers come from

The time conversions use the turn-value method published by USA Swimming and built into Hy-Tek Team Manager — the software most meets seed from. That means a 1.11 course factor for the 50, 100, 200 and 400 IM, 0.8925 and 1.02 for the distance events, and a fixed turn value per stroke.

The points calculator uses the official World Aquatics base times for the current scoring period, taken from World Aquatics' own published table rather than a third-party copy. Long course base times run for the calendar year; short course base times run from 1 September.

What these numbers are not

Every conversion is an estimate. The value of a turn is personal — a swimmer with a long underwater gains far more from a short course pool than one who surfaces early — so a converted time will usually land within about two to three per cent of what you would actually swim, not on it.

Converted times are not records, and championship meets normally want a time swum in the course they are run in. Check the meet information sheet before relying on one.

Corrections

If you spot a factor, a base time or a worked example that looks wrong, please get in touch. Mistakes in this kind of table are worth fixing quickly.
